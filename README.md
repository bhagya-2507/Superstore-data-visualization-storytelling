# Superstore Data visualization & Storytelling

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Sample Superstore dataset and transforming the data into meaningful visualizations and business insights using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard follows a storytelling approach to understand sales performance, profitability, sub-category performance, and regional differences.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective is to understand:

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Sales and profit trends over time
- Category and sub-category performance
- Regional profitability
- Opportunities for improving business performance

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Sample Superstore Dataset
- DAX calculated columns for date analysis

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Business Overview

Key metrics:

- Total Sales: 95.17K
- Total Profit: 8.57K
- Total Orders: 600

The analysis shows that Furniture has the highest sales, while Technology generates the highest profit.

### 2. Sales & Profit Trend

This page analyzes monthly sales and profit trends and compares annual sales with profit.

The analysis highlights that sales and profit do not always move in the same way.

### 3. Category & Sub-Category Analysis

This page analyzes profitability and sales across different sub-categories and regions.

Key observations include differences in sub-category profitability and regional performance.

### 4. Insights & Recommendations

The final page summarizes the major business insights and provides recommendations focused on:

- High-profit categories
- Low-profit sub-categories
- Regional performance improvement

## 💡 Key Insights

- Technology is the strongest profit contributor.
- Profitability varies significantly across sub-categories.
- The West region has the highest profit, while Central has the lowest.
- Higher sales do not always result in higher profit.

## 📌 Recommendations

1. Focus on high-profit categories and products.
2. Review low-profit sub-categories and investigate pricing, discounts, and costs.
3. Analyze weaker regional performance and identify opportunities to improve profitability.
